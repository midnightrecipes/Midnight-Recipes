Calabaza en Tacha Inspired Mexican Pumpkin Pie

Prep: 25 mins · Cook: 55–65 mins · Total: 5 hrs 20 mins+ · Serves: 6-8

Inspired by Coco, this Mexican-style pumpkin pie reimagines calabaza en tacha with whole-spice infused cream and a rich piloncillo-style glaze.

Mexico's Día de Muertos, the traditional celebration featured in the movie Coco, centers around remembering and welcoming back loved ones who have passed away.

Celebrated especially on November 1 and 2, families create vibrant ofrendas (altars) decorated with photographs, candles, marigolds, and the favorite foods that their loved ones enjoyed.

Growing up in Japan, our summer tradition of welcoming ancestral spirits during Obon (お盆) was always quiet, solemn, and reflective. I wasn't used to a tradition that celebrates and remembers the dead in such a bright, warm, and lively way. When I watched Coco, I found that perspective beautiful and special.

Calabaza en Tacha is a traditional Mexican seasonal pumpkin sweet often placed on these ofrendas. Traditionally, pieces of hard-shelled pumpkin are slowly simmered in a dark piloncillo (panela) syrup infused with whole cinnamon, cloves, star anise, and fresh orange until perfectly tender and glazed.

Tonight, I wanted to create something a little different from a standard pumpkin pie—a dessert warmly inspired by Calabaza en Tacha, taking a quiet moment late at night to remember the people I love. By transferring those traditional flavours into a pie format, using spice-infused cream and topping it with a glossy orange-molasses syrup, this pie comes together with a beautifully rounded flavour and a smooth, comforting texture.

Ingredients

PIE CRUST

CALABAZA FILLING

  • 400 g Pumpkin purée
  • 2 large Eggs
  • 180 ml 10% cream (Half & Half)
  • 55 g Dark brown sugar
  • 10 g Molasses
  • 15 ml Orange juice
  • 0.25 tsp Salt

SPICE INFUSION

  • 0.5 Cinnamon stick
  • 2 Whole cloves
  • 1 Star anise
  • 1 strip Orange peel, 1 inch / 2-3 cm long

THE PILONCILLO SYRUP

  • 38 g Dark brown sugar
  • 5 g Molasses
  • 30 ml Orange juice
  • 15 ml Water
  • 1 strip Orange peel, 1 inch / 2–3 cm long
  • 1 pinch Salt

Instructions

  1. MAKE THE CRUST

    Preheat your oven to 175°C / 350°F.

    Start with one of our usual recipes - Building Block Pie Crust (OPTION B — PARTIAL BLIND-BAKE).

  2. INFUSE THE SPICES

    Squeeze the orange juice and keep the orange peel strip aside for the spice infusion and syrup.

    Pour 180 ml 10% cream into a small saucepan.

    Add 1/2 cinnamon stick, 2 whole cloves, 1 star anise, and 1 strip of orange peel.

    Warm gently over low heat for 10 minutes. Do not let the Half & Half boil.

    Turn off the heat and let the spices steep for another 10 minutes.

    Remove the cinnamon stick, cloves, star anise, and orange peel. Prolonged steeping can make the peel bitter.

    While the infused Half & Half is still warm, whisk in 55 g dark brown sugar and 10 g molasses until completely dissolved.

  3. MAKE THE FILLING

    In a large bowl, whisk together 400 g pumpkin purée, 2 large eggs, 15 ml orange juice, and 2 g salt.

    Slowly pour in the warm spiced Half & Half mixture.

    Whisk gently until everything is smooth and evenly combined.

  4. BAKE

    Pour the pumpkin filling into the crust.

    Bake for 40–50 minutes.

    The edges should be set while the center still has a slight wobble when the pie is gently shaken.

    Remove from the oven.

    Let the pie cool slightly until it is no longer hot, then refrigerate.

    Don't worry if the center looks slightly soft when it first comes out. The custard will continue to set as it cools.

  5. FAKE THE PILONCILLO SYRUP

    Add 38 g dark brown sugar, 5 g molasses, 30 ml orange juice, 15 ml water, 1 strip of orange peel, and 1 g salt to a small saucepan.

    Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat.

    Cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sugar has dissolved and the syrup has thickened slightly.

    Remove the orange peel. Let the syrup cool slightly.

  6. FINISH

    Once the pie is no longer hot, refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.

    Before serving, spoon a thin layer of the piloncillo syrup over the surface. For the glossiest finish, warm the syrup slightly and drizzle it over the pie just before serving.
