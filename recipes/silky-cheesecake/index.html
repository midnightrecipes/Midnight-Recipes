Silky Cheesecake

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 20 mins · Cook: 45–50 mins · Total: 9 hrs 5 mins+ · Serves: 6-8

Inspired by Haruki Murakami’s Norwegian Wood, this smooth, custard-like cheesecake balances rich cream cheese with a gentle sour cream tang.

In Haruki Murakami’s Norwegian Wood, there’s a scene where Midori throws away a strawberry shortcake and asks for something else instead: “Chocolate mousse or cheesecake?” It’s a rather unusual request, but somehow, that little moment stayed with me. And it made me think about cheesecake.

For this recipe, I wanted to make a cheesecake that was rich and creamy, but not too dense or heavy. Something with a smooth, almost silky texture that you can enjoy one slice at a time — and that makes you want just one more bite. The cream cheese gives it its richness, while sour cream and a little lemon juice add a gentle tang that balances everything out. Baking it slowly in a water bath helps keep the texture soft and delicate. Once chilled, it holds its shape beautifully. But with each bite, it’s wonderfully creamy and almost melts in your mouth.

"To make it up to you, I'll go out and buy you something else. What would you like? Chocolate mousse? Cheesecake?"

I may not know whether Midori would have chosen this one, but if cheesecake is your answer, I hope you’ll give it a try tonight!

Ingredients

  • 250 g Cream cheese
  • 85 g Granulated sugar
  • 170 g Sour cream
  • 125 ml 35% whipping cream
  • 2 Eggs
  • 8 g Cornstarch
  • 10 ml Lemon juice
  • 0.5 tsp Vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. PREPARE THE INGREDIENTS

    Bring 250 g cream cheese, 170 g sour cream, and 2 eggs close to room temperature.

    Preheat your oven to 150°C / 300°F. Line a 7 inch / 18 cm loaf pan with parchment paper.

    Beat the eggs in a separate bowl and set aside.

  2. BUILD THE CREAMY BASE

    In a large bowl, mix 250 g cream cheese until completely smooth and free of lumps.

    Add the 85 g granulated sugar, 170 g sour cream, and 125 ml whipping cream. Mix until evenly combined.

  3. FINISH WITH A SILKY BATTER

    Gradually add 2 beaten eggs in two or three additions, mixing gently after each addition.

    Sift in the 8 g cornstarch, then add the 10 ml lemon juice and 3 ml vanilla extract. Mix until the batter is smooth and uniform.

    Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan. Gently tap the pan against the counter to release large air bubbles.

    For an extra-silky texture, strain the batter through a fine-mesh sieve before pouring it into the pan.

  4. LET THE WATER BATH DO THE WORK

    Place the loaf pan inside a larger, deep oven-safe tray. Carefully pour hot water into the outer tray to create a water bath.

    Bake at 150°C / 300°F for approximately 45–50 minutes, checking for doneness from around 45 minutes. The edges should be set while the center still jiggles slightly when the pan is gently moved.

    While the cheesecake bakes, you can go take a shower. Let the oven do the work for a while.

  5. GIVE IT THE NIGHT

    Remove the pan from the water bath and let the cheesecake cool in the pan until it reaches room temperature.

    Refrigerate overnight for at least 8 hours to allow the texture to set completely.

    Once thoroughly chilled, lift the cheesecake out of the pan.
