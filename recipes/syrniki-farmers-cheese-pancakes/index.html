Syrniki (Farmer's Cheese Pancakes)

Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 10 mins · Total: 40 mins · Serves: 4-5

Creamy, tender Eastern European cheese pancakes made with pressed cottage cheese (Twarog/Tvorog).

Cottage cheese has been gaining attention among health-conscious food lovers as a versatile, protein-rich ingredient. Across the internet, I came across countless creative ways to use it, from pancakes and baked goods to creamy desserts.

Among those discoveries, I found Syrniki, traditional Eastern European cheese pancakes.

To my surprise, I discovered that the farmer’s cheese traditionally used to make Syrniki is actually quite easy to find at regular grocery stores here in Canada. Apparently, I had walked past it thousands of times without ever noticing it!

That’s one of the things I love about trying new recipes: you often discover ingredients that have been right in front of you all along.

For my version, I wanted to keep the pancakes rich in cheese flavor and extra creamy, so I used less flour. The result is a wonderfully moist, tender center that pairs beautifully with both sweet and savory toppings.

Try them with berries, jam, or honey and sour cream for a sweet breakfast, or go savory with garlic and dill mayonnaise. They are going well with anything you like!

Ingredients

  • 500 g Pressed cottage cheese (Twarog/Tvorog)
  • 127 g Eggs
  • 62 g All-purpose flour
  • 50 g Granulated sugar
  • 1.7 g Salt
  • 2.8 g Vanilla extract
  • All-purpose flour, for shaping
  • Butter, for pan-frying

Instructions

  1. MIX THE BATTER

    Break up 500 g cheese gently with a fork. Add 127 g eggs, 50 g sugar, 2 g salt, and 3 g vanilla extract, and mix until combined while leaving a little texture.

    Add 62 g flour and mix just until incorporated. Avoid overmixing.

  2. REST THE BATTER

    Refrigerate the mixture for 20–30 minutes so the flour absorbs moisture and makes shaping easier.

  3. SHAPE AND PAN-FRY

    Lightly flour your hands and work surface with flour, divide into about 8 portions, and shape into rounds roughly 6–7 cm wide and 1–2 cm thick.

    Melt butter in a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat and cook for about 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through.

  4. SERVE AND ENJOY!

    Serve warm with sour cream, berries, jam, or honey, or pair with garlic and dill mayonnaise.'
