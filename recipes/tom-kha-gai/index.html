Tom Kha Gai (Coconut Chicken Soup)

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 20 mins · Total: 30 mins · Serves: 2–3

TasteAtlas’s top chicken soup tested! A velvety Thai coconut broth infused with galangal, lemongrass, makrut lime, and gentle chili heat.

TasteAtlas had ranked Tom Kha Gai #1 on its list of the world’s best chicken soups. I was already familiar with Tom Yum, but somehow Tom Kha Gai had always stayed in the background for me.

It sounded like a dream combination: creamy coconut milk, the bright citrus of makrut lime leaf and lemongrass, fresh lime acidity, and the gentle heat and peppery bite of chilies and galangal.

The biggest challenge was simply finding all the ingredients. The actual cooking is surprisingly quick and simple, and in a diverse city like Toronto, hunting down those fresh Asian herbs isn't as difficult as you might think.

When I took my first sip, I instantly understood why it earned that top spot. It’s an incredible balance of contrasts: the soothing, rich coconut milk hits you first, followed by the sharp citrus of makrut lime and lemongrass, the woody-peppery warmth of fresh galangal, and finally a clean, bright sour kick from fresh lime juice. It’s rich, velvety, sour, and mildly spicy all in one spoon.

If the internet was calling it the world’s best chicken soup, I just had to find out for myself even at midnight…!

Ingredients

CHICKEN & COCONUT BASE

  • 227 g Chicken thighs, boneless, skinless
  • 150 g Mushrooms
  • 400 ml Coconut milk
  • 240 ml Water or chicken stock

AROMATICS

  • 30 g Galangal, thinly sliced
  • 1–2 stalks Lemongrass, bruised and cut into pieces
  • 4–5 leaves Makrut lime leaves, rehydrated if dried
  • -30 g Onion, sliced
  • 5 g Garlic, lightly crushed
  • 2–4 Fresh chilies, lightly crushed

SEASONING

  • 30 ml Fish sauce
  • 30 ml Fresh lime juice
  • 4 g Palm sugar or granulated sugar
  • 2–3 g Salt

TOPPING (optional)

  • Fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. BUILD THE AROMATIC BROTH

    If using dried 4–5 leaves makrut lime leaves, soak them in hot water for 5–10 minutes. Drain and gently tear them.

    Thinly slice 30 g galangal. Remove the tough outer layers from 1–2 stalks lemongrass stalks, bruise them with the back of a knife, and cut them into 5–6 cm pieces.

    If using minced lemongrass instead, measure 1½ tbsp.

    Add 240 ml water or chicken stock, galangal, lemongrass, makrut lime leaves, -30 g onion, 5 g garlic, and 2–4 fresh chilies to a saucepan.

    Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat and cook for 5–10 minutes, until the broth smells deeply aromatic.

  2. COOK THE CHICKEN & COCONUT

    Cut 227 g chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces.

    Add the chicken thighs to the aromatic broth and simmer until just cooked through.

    Add 150 g mushrooms and cook for another 2–3 minutes.

    Pour in 400 ml coconut milk. Reduce the heat to low and warm gently.

    Do not let the coconut milk boil aggressively. This prevents the coconut milk emulsion from splitting.

  3. SEASON & FINISH

    Stir in 30 ml fish sauce and 4 g palm sugar or granulated sugar. Turn off the heat.

    Add 30 ml fresh lime juice.

    Taste and adjust with a little more fish sauce for saltiness, lime juice for acidity, or sugar if the flavours feel too sharp.

    Finish with fresh cilantro.
