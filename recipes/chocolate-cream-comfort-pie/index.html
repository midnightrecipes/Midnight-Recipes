Chocolate Cream Comfort Pie

Rated 5.0 / 5 (2 reviews)

Prep: 15 mins · Cook: 15 mins · Total: 4 hrs 30 min · Serves: 6-8

Inspired by Julie & Julia, this silky dark chocolate cream pie features a rich espresso-kissed custard in a crisp graham cracker crust—pure midnight comfort.

This homemade chocolate cream pie features a rich, silky dark chocolate custard filling made with real chocolate, cocoa powder, and a hint of espresso, all nestled in a crisp graham cracker crust.

Inspired by Julie & Julia, this pie is more than just a dessert. Reine de Saba (chocolate cake with sliced almonds) might be the most famous dessert from the film, but honestly, Julie’s whole emotional breakdown over making a chocolate cream pie is what totally spoke to me. No matter how messy or exhausting the day gets, the kitchen is always my favorite place to unwind.

It’s kind of funny, but the more tired I am, the more I actually want to bake late at night. There’s just something so comforting about tuning everything out and focusing on simple things—whisking milk, watching chocolate slowly melt, and just letting the night quiet down. Tonight, this pie was exactly what I needed. Rich, super chocolatey, and pure comfort in every bite.

The real secret here... Just use a dark chocolate that you’d happily eat straight out of the wrapper. It gives you this incredibly smooth, rich filling that isn't crazy sweet. Plus, it’s one of those desserts people always get excited about—seeing friends take that first bite and smile makes all the late-night whisking 100% worth it!

Ingredients

CRUST

  • 200 g Graham crackers, finely crushed
  • 76 g Unsalted butter, melted

CHOCOLATE FILLING

  • 150 g Dark chocolate, chopped
  • 2 g Espresso powder
  • 24 g Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 72 g Egg yolks
  • 75 g Granulated sugar
  • 4 Tbsp Cornstarch
  • 1 pinch Salt
  • 500 ml Whole milk
  • 21 g Unsalted butter

TOPPING (optional)

  • 250 ml 35% whipping cream
  • 38 g Granulated sugar
  • Cocoa powder, cacao nibs, or flaky sea salt, for finishing

Instructions

  1. CRUSH IT DOWN

    Preheat your oven to 175°C / 350°F.

    Crush 200 g graham crackers finely.

    Food Processor (Fastest): Pulse for about 30 seconds.

    Quiet Mode (Silent): Seal in a zip-top bag and roll with a rolling pin—perfect for late-night baking.

    Add 76 g melted butter to the crushed graham crackers and mix until the crumbs are evenly coated.

    Press the crumb mixture firmly into a 9 inch / 23 cm pie dish using the flat bottom of a cup.

    Bake for 8–10 minutes. Let it cool.

  2. MAKE IT GLOSSY

    In a saucepan, whisk 75 g sugar, 32 g cornstarch, 24 g cocoa powder, 2 g espresso powder, and 1 g salt.

    Slowly pour in 500 ml whole milk, whisking thoroughly until completely smooth before turning on the heat.

    Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly until the mixture bubbles and thickens into a pudding-like consistency.

    Remove the pan from the heat.

  3. MAKE IT SILKY

    In a separate bowl, whisk 72 g egg yolks.

    Slowly whisk half of the hot chocolate cream into the yolks to warm them up, then pour the yolk mixture back into the pan.

    Return to low heat for 1 minute, whisking constantly until thick and glossy.

    Remove from heat, then stir in 150 g chopped dark chocolate and 21 g butter until fully melted and smooth.

  4. POUR AND WAIT

    Pour the warm chocolate filling into your chilled pie crust.

    Tap the pie dish gently on the counter 2–3 times to level the surface and release trapped air bubbles.

    Refrigerate for at least 4 hours. Overnight works best.

  5. WHIP IT LATE

    Whip 250 ml cold 35% whipping cream to soft peaks.

    Gradually add 38 g granulated sugar and whip until medium-firm peaks form.

    Dollop generously over the chilled pie and finish with cocoa powder, cacao nibs, or flaky sea salt.
