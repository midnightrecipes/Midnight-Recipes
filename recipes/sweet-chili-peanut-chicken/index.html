Sweet Chili Peanut Chicken

Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 15 mins · Total: 25 mins · Serves: 2

Crisp chicken thighs tossed in a homemade sweet chili, peanut butter, and garlic sauce inspired by street food in Thailand.

I first fell in love with the combination of sweet chili sauce and peanuts in Thailand. They were served alongside crispy Thai fish cakes (Tod Mun Pla), and honestly, I think I enjoyed the sauce just as much as the fish cakes, if not more.

It was sweet, tangy, a little spicy, and had that nice crunch from the crushed peanuts. Such a simple combination, but I couldn't stop thinking about it even after I got back home.

Thailand isn't exactly around the corner from Toronto, and going back just for that sauce wasn't really an option. So one night, with some chicken sitting in the fridge, I started wondering if I could bring those flavours into a dish of my own.

I decided to give it a try.

This isn't a traditional Thai dish, and I'm not trying to recreate the original fish cakes. I just wanted to bring back a flavour I really liked and see what I could make with it.

And that's how this recipe came about. Sometimes, it's not the dish itself that stays with you, but a flavour you just can't forget.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 454 g Chicken Thighs, Cut Into Bite-Sized Pieces
  • 24 g Cornstarch
  • 3 g Salt
  • 1 g Black Pepper
  • 30 ml Neutral Oil, for Pan-Frying

Sweet Chili Peanut Sauce

  • 32 g 100% Peanut Butter
  • 45 ml Water
  • 30 ml Rice Vinegar
  • 25 g Granulated Sugar
  • 5 ml Soy Sauce
  • 5 g Sambal Oelek
  • 5 g Garlic, Finely Grated
  • 5 g Fresh Ginger, Finely Grated
  • 5 ml Sesame Oil
  • 1.5 g Salt, or to taste

Garnish

  • Roasted Peanuts, Roughly Crushed
  • Fresh Cilantro, Chopped
  • 1–2 Lime Wedges

Instructions

  1. CRISP THE CHICKEN

    Pat the 454 g chicken thighs dry and cut them into bite-sized pieces.

    Toss the chicken with the 24 g cornstarch, 3 g salt, and 1 g black pepper until evenly coated.

    Heat the 30 ml neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

    Arrange the chicken in a single layer. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Work in batches if needed.

    Transfer the chicken to a plate, leaving about 1–2 teaspoons of oil in the skillet.

  2. BUILD THE SAUCE

    Reduce the heat to medium-low.

    Add the 5 g sambal oelek, 5 g garlic, and 5 g fresh ginger to the skillet. Sauté for 20–30 seconds, stirring gently. Do not let the garlic burn.

    Add the 32 g peanut butter, 45 ml water, 30 ml rice vinegar, 25 g granulated sugar, 5 ml soy sauce, and 2 g salt.

    Stir for 1–2 minutes until the sauce becomes smooth and glossy.

    Stir in the 5 ml sesame oil.

    Taste and adjust the seasoning. Add a little more salt only if needed. If the sauce is too thick, add water a teaspoon at a time.

  3. COAT THE CHICKEN

    Return the crispy chicken to the skillet.

    Toss gently for 1–2 minutes until the pieces are evenly coated.

    If the sauce is too thick, add 5–10 ml water and toss again. This is extra adjustment water, separate from the sauce water listed in the ingredients.

    Remove from the heat once the chicken is coated in the glossy sauce.

  4. GARNISH AND SERVE

    Sprinkle with roughly crushed roasted peanuts and chopped cilantro.

    Serve with lime wedges for squeezing over the chicken just before eating.
