Classic Pumpkin Pie

Rated 5.0 / 5 (2 reviews)

Prep: 20 mins · Cook: 35–45 mins · Total: 2 hrs 55 mins+ · Serves: 6-8

A Canadian Thanksgiving classic featuring a silky pumpkin filling and a custom warm autumn spice blend baked in a flaky butter crust.

Pumpkin pie is the Canadian Thanksgiving classic, and I had my very first bite on Thanksgiving Monday back in October during my first year in Canada. Safe to say, I was completely hooked! The smooth, silky pumpkin filling and that warm punch of cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg were unlike anything I knew back home with Japanese Kabocha. After that, I went a little crazy trying pumpkin pie everywhere I could get my hands on. Fast forward through endless pie runs and tweaking ingredients, I’ve finally nailed my dream spice mix. I make it all the time now. Especially, baking it in the middle of a quiet autumn night hits differently.

Ingredients

PIE CRUST

PUMPKIN FILLING

  • 400 g Pumpkin purée
  • 70 g Brown sugar
  • 2 large Eggs
  • 80 ml Whole milk
  • 80 ml 35% whipping cream
  • 1 tsp Ground cinnamon
  • 0.5 tsp Ground ginger
  • 0.5 g Ground nutmeg
  • 0.25 g Ground cloves
  • 0.25 tsp Salt

Instructions

  1. MAKE THE CRUST

    Preheat your oven to 175°C / 350°F.

    Start with one of our usual recipes — Building Block Pie Crust (OPTION B — PARTIAL BLIND-BAKE).

  2. MAKE THE FILLING

    Mix 400 g pumpkin purée, 70 g brown sugar, 2 large eggs, 80 ml whole milk, 80 ml whipping cream, 3 g ground cinnamon, 1 g ground ginger, 1/2 g ground nutmeg, 1/4 g ground cloves, and 2 g salt until smooth.

  3. POUR & BAKE

    Pour the filling into the warm crust.

    Bake at 175°C / 350°F for 35–40 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still has a slight wobble.

  4. COOL & CHILL

    Cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Slice chilled and serve.
