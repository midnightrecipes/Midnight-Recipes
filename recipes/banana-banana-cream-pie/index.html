Banana Banana Cream Pie

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 20 mins · Cook: 30 mins · Total: 3 hrs 50 mins+ · Serves: 6-8

Unapologetically banana-y! Overripe bananas cooked down into a rich, caramel-like custard inside a custom spiced biscuit crust.

A few nearly black bananas had been sitting on my counter for way too long. They were way past the point where I'd normally eat them fresh, but as it turns out, they were absolute perfection for this pie.

I started looking through traditional banana cream pie recipes and noticed that most of them rely on a few slices of fresh banana hiding underneath a mountain of standard vanilla custard.

I wanted a pie that was seriously, unapologetically banana-y. Not vanilla pudding with a vague hint of banana flavour, but a rich, velvety banana custard made from real fruit.

So I took those overripe bananas, cooked them down in a skillet until they were thick, dark, and sweet, and blended them right into the custard. Then, I tucked fresh banana halves underneath for a little bite and that beautiful, clean cross-section when you slice into it.

To complement all that fruitiness, I crafted a custom spiced biscuit crust. The warm, exotic notes of cinnamon, ginger, and nutmeg cut through the rich sweetness of the banana, creating a beautiful depth of flavour.

Basically, this pie is my answer to one very simple, late-night question: What if we just stopped treating the banana like an afterthought and made the banana the custard itself?

Ingredients

CRUST

  • 200 g Plain biscuits, finely crushed
  • 2 g Ground cinnamon
  • 1 g Ground ginger
  • 1 pinch Ground nutmeg
  • 1.5 g Salt
  • 80 g Unsalted butter, melted

FILLING

  • 250 g VERY ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 Firm ripe bananas, cut in half lengthwise
  • 400 ml Whole milk
  • 54 g Egg yolks
  • 35 g Granulated sugar
  • 32 g Cornstarch
  • 5 ml Vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch Salt

TOPPING (Optional)

  • 180 ml 35% whipping cream
  • 38 g Granulated sugar
  • Cinnamon, for finishing
  • Sliced Banana, for finishing

Instructions

  1. TURN IT UP & MAKE IT JAMMY

    Preheat your oven to 175°C / 350°F.

    Cook 250 g very ripe mashed bananas in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat for 8–12 minutes, stirring often.

    Keep cooking until the moisture cooks off and the banana turns thick, glossy, and jammy.

    No sugar. No butter. Just banana. Set aside.

  2. FAKE THE BISCOFF

    Crush 200 g plain biscuits finely.

    Food Processor (Fastest): Pulse for about 30 seconds.

    Quiet Mode (Silent): Seal in a zip-top bag and roll with a rolling pin—perfect for late-night baking.

    Mix with 2 g cinnamon, 1 g ginger, 1 g nutmeg, 2 g salt, and 80 g melted butter.

    Press firmly into a 9 inch / 23 cm pie dish.

    Bake for 8–10 minutes, then let cool.

  3. MAKE IT THICK

    Whisk 35 g sugar, 32 g cornstarch, and 1 g salt in a saucepan.

    Gradually whisk in 400 ml whole milk until smooth.

    Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until the custard becomes very thick and starts to bubble.

    Keep whisking and cook for another 1–2 minutes after it starts bubbling.

    The whisk should leave a clear trail, and the custard should slowly fill it back in.

  4. WARM THE EGGS

    Whisk 54 g egg yolks in a bowl.

    Slowly add the hot custard, a little at a time, whisking constantly.

    Pour everything back into the saucepan.

    Return to low heat and cook for 1 minute, whisking constantly, until thick and glossy.

    Remove from the heat.

  5. BLEND THE BANANA IN

    Add the concentrated banana and 5 ml vanilla extract to the warm custard.

    Blend until completely smooth and silky.

    Quiet Mode (Silent): Want a little texture? Skip the food processor and stir it in by hand.

  6. HIDE THE BANANAS & NOW WE WAIT

    Peel 2 firm-ripe bananas and cut each one in half lengthwise.

    Arrange the banana halves along the outer edge of the cooled crust, flat-side down.

    Pour the banana cream over the top and smooth the surface.

    Refrigerate for at least 4 hours. Overnight is even better!

  7. TOP IT OFF

    Whip 180 ml cold heavy cream to soft peaks. Add 38 g sugar and whip to medium-firm peaks.

    Dollop over the chilled pie and finish with cinnamon and sliced banana, if you like.
