Khai Yat Sai (Thai Stuffed Omelette)

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 15 mins · Cook: 20 mins · Total: 35 mins · Serves: 4

A traditional Thai stuffed omelette wrapping savoury ground pork, tomato, and onion in a soft egg sheet with crushed peanuts.

Many food cultures have their own version of an egg-wrapped dish. Japan has omurice, China has dan jiao, and Italy has involtini di frittata. Thailand has Khai Yat Sai (ไข่ยัดไส้), a traditional Thai stuffed omelette filled with savoury ground pork and vegetables.

For this version, I cook ground pork with garlic, onion and tomato, then season it with fish sauce and oyster sauce. The savoury filling is wrapped in a thin, soft omelette and finished with fresh cilantro and crushed peanuts.

When you cut into it, the thin omelette yields to a fragrant, savoury filling of ground pork and vegetables, all tied together by the umami of fish sauce and oyster sauce. It hits every note at once: savoury, slightly sweet, tangy and satisfyingly juicy!

It's simple, satisfying, and a lovely reminder that a little creativity with everyday ingredients can make something special.

Ingredients

PORK FILLING

  • 454 g Ground pork (regular)
  • 140 g Onion, finely diced
  • 110 g Tomato, finely diced
  • 10 g Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp Oyster sauce
  • 2 tsp Granulated sugar
  • Neutral oil, for greasing the pan
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

OMELETTE

  • 400 g Eggs
  • 1 tbsp Water (optional)
  • Neutral oil, for greasing the pan

TO SERVE

  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish
  • Peanuts, crushed, for garnish

Instructions

  1. COOK THE FILLING

    Finely chop 10 g garlic and 140 g onion. Dice 110 g tomato into small pieces.

    Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and onion and cook for 2–3 minutes, until softened and fragrant.

    Add 454 g ground pork and break it up with a spatula. Cook until no longer pink.

  2. SEASON AND REDUCE

    Add 15 ml fish sauce, 15 ml oyster sauce and 8 g sugar. Stir well to coat the pork.

    Add the diced tomato and cook for another 3–5 minutes, until softened and most of the liquid has evaporated.

    Taste and adjust with salt and black pepper if needed. Set aside to cool slightly.

  3. MAKE THE OMELETTE

    This recipe makes 200 individual omelettes. Cook them one at a time, using 2 eggs for each omelette in an 8 inch / 20 cm frying pane.

    Beat 400 g eggs in a bowl. Add 15 ml water if you prefer a slightly softer texture.

    Lightly grease an 8 inch / 20 cm non-stick frying pan with oil. Heat over medium-low heat.

    Pour in the eggs and gently swirl the pan to create a thin, even layer.

    Cook until the bottom is set and the surface is just beginning to set but still soft. Turn off the heat. Avoid browning the egg.

    Repeat with the remaining eggs to make the omelettes in the same way.

  4. FILL AND FOLD

    Divide the pork filling into 200 equal portions. Place one portion in the centre of each omelette, leaving approximately 2 inches / 5 cm of space around the edges.

    Fold the top and bottom edges towards the centre, then fold in the left and right sides to create a neat square parcel.

  5. FLIP AND REVEAL

    Place a serving plate upside down over the frying pan. Carefully flip the pan and plate together to turn the omelette onto the plate.

    Using a sharp knife, cut an X in the centre of the omelette. Gently open the four flaps to reveal the filling.

    Finish with fresh cilantro and crushed peanuts, and serve warm.
