Fun(Key) Lime Pie

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 20 mins · Cook: 30 mins · Total: 4 hrs 50 mins+ · Serves: 6-8

A brilliant (Key) Lime Pie alternative made with easy-to-find Persian limes, extra zest, sour cream, and a fluffy toasted meringue.

Key Lime Pie is everywhere in Toronto. I kept seeing it in grocery store freezer sections and bakery display cases, almost as if it were just another everyday dessert.

One night, I decided to make one myself and went looking for actual Key limes. And suddenly, they were nowhere to be found.

The pie was easy to find. The Key limes were not.

So I stopped hunting for the elusive Key lime and worked with what I could actually buy: standard Persian limes.

A traditional Key Lime Pie is a clash of extremes—intensely sharp lime sourness meeting heavy, syrup-sweet condensed milk. The first time I ever tried an authentic slice, my immediate thought was, “Wow, everything about this is SO strong... this is THE classic American flavour!”

To make it a little milder and more refined for my late-night palate, I brought in sour cream for extra body and a soft, smooth tang.

It isn't a traditional Key Lime Pie, but it delivers all the creamy, tart, and sun-bright character I was craving. It’s a Key Lime Pie-inspired dessert, made with the limes you can actually find!

Ingredients

GRAHAM CRACKER CRUST

  • 200 g Graham crackers, finely crushed
  • 76 g Unsalted butter, melted

LIME FILLING

  • 300 ml Sweetened condensed milk
  • 54 g Egg yolks
  • 58 g Sour cream
  • 120 ml Fresh lime juice
  • 6 g Lime zest, finely grated

MERINGUE

  • 90 g Egg whites
  • 50 g Granulated sugar
  • 0.5 g Cream of tartar

Instructions

  1. CRUSH IT DOWN

    Preheat your oven to 175°C / 350°F.

    Crush 200 g graham crackers finely.

    Food Processor (Fastest): Pulse for about 30 seconds.

    Quiet Mode: Seal in a zip-top bag and roll with a rolling pin—perfect for late-night baking.

    Add 76 g melted butter to the crushed graham crackers and mix until the crumbs are evenly coated.

    Press the crumb mixture firmly into a 9 inch / 23 cm pie dish using the flat bottom of a cup.

    Bake for 8–10 minutes. Let it cool.

  2. MIX THE LIME FILLING

    Finely grate 6 g zest from the limes, then squeeze enough limes to obtain 120 ml fresh lime juice.

    Separate the eggs, placing the 54 g egg yolks in a mixing bowl and reserving the 90 g egg whites for the meringue.

    Add the 300 ml sweetened condensed milk, 58 g sour cream, lime zest, and lime juice to the egg yolks.

    Whisk everything together until completely smooth.

  3. BAKE THE FILLING

    Pour the lime filling into the cooled graham cracker crust.

    Bake at 175°C / 350°F for 10 minutes.

    The edges should be beginning to set while the center still has a gentle jiggle.

    Remove the pie from the oven and let it rest for 5–10 minutes while preparing the meringue.

  4. WHIP THE MERINGUE

    Place the 90 g egg whites in a completely clean and dry bowl. Beat until foamy.

    Add 1/2 g cream of tartar.

    Gradually add the 50 g granulated sugar, a little at a time, while continuing to beat.

    Continue beating until the meringue is glossy and holds stiff peaks.

  5. MAKE THE PEAKS

    Spoon the meringue over the warm lime filling.

    Spread the meringue all the way to the edge of the crust, making sure it completely touches and seals against the filling and crust edge. Leaving zero gaps is crucial to prevent the meringue from shrinking or weeping liquid later.

    Gently touch the back of a spoon to the meringue and pull straight up to build rustic, airy peaks.

  6. BROWN THE MERINGUE & CHILL

    Return the pie to the 175°C / 350°F oven for 5–10 minutes, until the peaks are lightly golden.

    Watch closely near the end because the tips can brown quickly.

    Refrigerate for at least 4 hours before slicing.
