Cowboy BBQ Sauce

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 5 mins · Cook: 20 mins · Total: 25 mins · Serves: About 1 cup

A quick, smoky, and tangy-sweet homemade BBQ sauce powered by apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, molasses, and smoked paprika.

Ingredients

  • 204 g Ketchup
  • 60 ml Apple cider Vinegar
  • 60 ml Water
  • 15 g Brown sugar
  • 10 ml Molasses
  • 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 g Mustard powder
  • 2 g Ground cumin
  • 1.5 g Garlic powder
  • 1.25 g Onion powder
  • 1.1 g Smoked paprika
  • 1.25 g Black pepper
  • 3 g Salt

Instructions

  1. EVERYTHING IN!

    Add 204 g ketchup, 60 ml apple cider vinegar, 60 ml water, 15 g brown sugar, 10 ml molasses, 15 ml Worcestershire sauce, 2 g mustard powder, 2 g ground cumin, 2 g garlic powder, 1 g onion powder, 1 g smoked paprika, 1 g black pepper, and 3 g salt to a small saucepan.

    Bring to a gentle simmer over low to medium-low heat.

    Simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.

    Let cool before using.
