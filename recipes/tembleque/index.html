Puerto Rican Coconut Pudding (Tembleque)

Rated 5.0 / 5 (1 review)

Prep: 5 mins · Cook: 10 mins · Total: 4 hrs 15 mins+ · Serves: 6

An easy vegan Puerto Rican coconut pudding, traditionally enjoyed during Christmas and New Year's celebrations. This creamy dessert has a rich coconut flavour and a soft, wobbly texture.

It's the holiday season!

Tembleque is one of Puerto Rico's beloved traditional holiday desserts, especially enjoyed during Christmas and New Year's celebrations. Puerto Rico is a vibrant island in the northeastern Caribbean, part of the Greater Antilles.

The name "tembleque" comes from the Spanish verb "temblar," meaning "to tremble" or "to shake." Once chilled and unmolded, this coconut pudding gently wobbles when you move the plate. That's exactly what makes it so special!

Interestingly, tembleque has a long history as a holiday treat. It was already mentioned in an 18th-century account of Puerto Rican Christmas celebrations, where it appeared among the foods served during festive visits between families.

What I love about this dessert is how simple it is to make. Just mix the ingredients, cook until thickened, pour into molds, and let the refrigerator do the rest! Its creamy coconut flavour and delicate, jiggly texture make it a wonderful addition to any holiday table.

If you're planning a Christmas party but don't have much time to prepare an elaborate dessert, this easy make-ahead recipe is perfect. You can make it the day before and simply unmold and garnish it when you're ready to serve!

Ingredients

  • 500 ml Coconut milk, full-fat
  • 65 g Granulated Sugar
  • 32 g Cornstarch
  • 1 pinch Salt
  • 1 Cinnamon stick
  • 5 ml Vanilla extract

TOPPING (optional)

  • Ground cinnamon
  • Shredded coconut, unsweetened

Instructions

  1. MAKE THE MIXTURE

    Set aside about 1/3 cup / 100 ml out of 500 ml coconut milk. Add 32 g cornstarch and whisk until completely smooth with no lumps.

    Add 65 g sugar and 1 g salt to the cornstarch mixture and stir to combine. Pour in the remaining coconut milk and add 1 cinnamon stick.

  2. COOK UNTIL THICKENED

    Place the saucepan over medium heat and cook, stirring constantly with a whisk. Continue stirring until the mixture becomes thick and smooth. Once thickened, cook for another minute to make sure the cornstarch is fully cooked.

  3. ADD THE VANILLA & FILL THE MOLD

    Remove the cinnamon stick and stir in 5 ml vanilla extract.

    Quickly divide the warm mixture among the muffin molds, as it sets quickly. Cover with plastic wrap and gently smooth the tops.

  4. CHILL UNTIL SET

    Let the pudding cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, until completely set.

  5. GARNISH & SERVE

    Unmold and dust with ground cinnamon and sprinkle with shredded coconut before serving.
